UCSD 2025-26 Identity — What the Data Says
✓ Strengths

Defense was elite: 2nd in conf DE (100.4), 2nd in 3PT% allowed (31.9%), strong steal rate (10.8, tied 3rd).

Ball security: 3rd in conf TO% (17.0) — Beattie and Beath protect the ball.

Net rating +4.30 was 3rd in conference — genuinely a top-3 team on both sides of the ball when healthy.

✗ Problems to Fix

Worst 3PT% in conference (30.9%) — spacing crisis was real. Chaikin is the only reliable shooter returning.

Dead last in OReb% (11th, 25.6%) — no second-chance points. Suutela (OR% 7.4) directly addresses this.

Slow tempo (69.6, 10th) — CSUN pushed pace and exploited it 3 times. Need more athleticism in transition.
